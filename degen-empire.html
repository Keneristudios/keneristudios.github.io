Frequently Asked Questions

Got Questions?

How do I join the Closed Beta on Google Play?

Send an email to keneristudios@gmail.com with your Google Play email address. We will add you to the authorized internal testing track within 24 hours.

Is real money used in the game?

No. All cash, stocks, crypto, and assets in the game are strictly simulated virtual items with zero real-world monetary equivalence. Real money is only used for optional VIP store memberships processed via Google Play.

Can I restore my VIP purchase on a new device?

Yes. Tap "Restore Purchases" in the VIP store modal. As long as you are signed into the same Google Play account, your purchase will be verified and restored immediately.

How do I delete my cloud save and leaderboard profile?

Email keneristudios@gmail.com with your registered Google email or in-game username. We will purge all associated Firestore documents within 30 days.
