Privacy Policy

Application: Degen Empire

Developer: Keneri Studios

Effective Date: August 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Keneri Studios handles user information in our mobile game, Degen Empire ("the Game").

1. Information We Collect Directly

Keneri Studios does not directly collect, store, or transmit any personal identification information (such as your legal name, email, phone number, location address, or banking credentials). We do not maintain any user databases or private servers containing your information.

2. Third-Party Services & Automated Data Collection

While we do not collect personal data directly, the Game uses third-party Software Development Kits (SDKs) to serve advertisements and support game stability. These providers may automatically collect non-personally identifiable technical information, such as:

  • Unique Advertising Identifiers (e.g., Google Advertising ID, Apple IDFA).
  • IP address and approximate geographic location (country/city level).
  • Device information (device model, operating system version, system language).
  • In-game ad performance data (impressions, clicks, views).

To learn more about how our third-party advertising partners handle your data, please review their privacy policies:

  • Google Play Services & Google AdMob
  • Unity Ads

3. Game Progress & Local Storage

Your game progress, upgrades, and currency are saved locally on your device (or synced via official platform cloud services like Google Play Games / Apple Game Center). Keneri Studios does not access or alter your save files remotely.

4. Children’s Online Privacy (COPPA & GDPR)

Our game is not directed at children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in the European Economic Area). We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children.

5. Managing Ad Personalization

You can opt out of interest-based advertising or reset your ad ID at any time via your device settings:

  • Android: Settings > Google > Ads > Delete or Reset advertising ID.
  • iOS: Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions or feedback regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact us at:

Email: contact@keneristudios.com
