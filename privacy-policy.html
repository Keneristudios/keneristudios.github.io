Master Privacy Policy

Transparency, security, and player-first privacy standards across all products.

Publisher / Developer: Keneri Studios

Applies to: All games, mobile applications, and web services (including Degen Empire: Richbro Edition)

Official Contact: keneristudios@gmail.com

Effective Date: September 2026

1. Scope of This Policy

This Master Privacy Policy applies to all mobile games, software applications, and web services published by Keneri Studios (collectively, "our Games, Apps, and Services"). By downloading, testing, or using any of our titles—including pre-release closed betas—you acknowledge the practices outlined herein.

2. Data We Collect & How We Use It

Keneri Studios adheres strictly to data minimization principles. We only collect the minimal information necessary to deliver core application functionality:

  • Account & Authentication Information (Optional): If you choose to connect with Google Sign-In to back up your progress across devices, we receive your Google account email address, display name, and unique User ID (UID) via Google Firebase Authentication. This information is used strictly for account management and cloud save synchronization.
  • In-Game Profile & Leaderboard: Your chosen alias, avatar, and total simulated fortune may be uploaded to our public Forbes Leaderboard powered by Google Cloud Firestore.
  • Purchase History: If you purchase an optional VIP pass or virtual package, we process the transaction receipt and product identifier (SKU) via Google Play Billing to activate your benefits and restore purchases. We never receive or store your payment card or banking information.
  • Gameplay & Local Storage: Game progress, assets, vehicles, and portfolio data are stored locally on your device (encrypted via MMKV) and mirrored to secure Google Cloud Firestore when cloud sync is active.

3. Third-Party Services & Automated SDK Collection

To support application stability, deliver optional rewarded advertising, and maintain leaderboards, our applications integrate trusted third-party Software Development Kits (SDKs). These third parties may automatically collect telemetry and technical identifiers:

  • Google Mobile Ads (AdMob): Collects device advertising identifiers (Google Advertising ID / GAID), ad interaction metrics (views, clicks), and coarse (approximate) location derived from IP addresses to serve contextual or personalized advertisements.
  • Google Firebase (Authentication & Cloud Firestore): Manages secure user authentication, cloud saves, and real-time leaderboard data. All data is encrypted in transit using industry-standard TLS/HTTPS protocols.
  • Google Play In-App Billing: Handles transaction verification, purchase token validation, and entitlement restoration.

You can review the privacy policies of our third-party infrastructure providers:

  • Google Privacy & Terms
  • Google AdMob Privacy Policy
  • Google Firebase Privacy & Security

4. Information We Do NOT Collect

We believe in strict data protection. We do not collect or request:

  • Precise GPS location data.
  • Physical addresses, telephone numbers, or biometric data.
  • Device contacts, text messages (SMS), photos, microphone, or camera access.
  • Credit card numbers, bank details, or financial accounts (handled solely by Google Play).

We do not sell, rent, monetize, or trade any player or user data to third-party data brokers.

5. Data Retention & Deletion Rights

We retain cloud save and leaderboard information only as long as you maintain an active account with our services. You have the full right to delete your data at any time:

  • Local Data: You can delete all local data immediately by clearing app storage or uninstalling the application from your device.
  • Cloud Save & Leaderboard Deletion: To permanently delete your Google account record, cloud saves, and leaderboard entries from our Firebase servers, email us at keneristudios@gmail.com with the subject "Data Deletion Request" and your in-app alias or registered Google email. We will process and permanently purge your records within 30 business days.

6. Children’s Online Privacy (COPPA & GDPR)

Our applications are designed for general audiences and are not directed at children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in the European Economic Area). We do not knowingly collect personal data from minors. If a parent or guardian discovers that personal data has been submitted inadvertently, please contact us immediately at keneristudios@gmail.com so we can remove the data.

7. Managing Advertising Preferences & Opt-Outs

You have full control over personalized advertising on your mobile device:

  • Android: Go to Settings > Google > Ads > Reset or Delete advertising ID.
  • iOS: Go to Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking and toggle app tracking permissions.
