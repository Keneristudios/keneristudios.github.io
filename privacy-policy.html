Master Privacy Policy

Transparency, security, and player-first privacy standards.

Publisher / Developer: Keneri Studios

Applies to: All games, prototypes, and services (including Degen Empire: Richbro Edition)

Official Contact: keneristudios@gmail.com

Effective Date: August 2026

1. Scope of This Policy

This Master Privacy Policy applies to all mobile games, software applications, and web services published by Keneri Studios (collectively, "our Games" or "the Services"). By downloading, testing, or playing any of our titles—including pre-release closed betas—you acknowledge the practices outlined herein.

2. Information We Do NOT Collect Directly

Keneri Studios firmly believes in data minimization. We do not operate private servers to store personal user identities. Specifically:

  • We do not collect or store your legal name, physical address, phone number, or personal email address inside our game code.
  • We do not require private account creation or passwords to play our games.
  • We do not sell, rent, monetize, or trade any player data to external data brokers.

3. Third-Party Services & Automated SDK Collection

To support game stability, deliver advertising, and monitor game performance, our Games integrate trusted third-party Software Development Kits (SDKs). These third parties may automatically collect non-personally identifiable technical telemetry, including:

  • Device Identifiers: Google Advertising ID (GAID), Apple IDFA, and unique vendor IDs.
  • Hardware Information: Device model, operating system version, CPU architecture, screen resolution, and system language.
  • Coarse Location: General country- or region-level data derived from IP addresses (not precise GPS location).
  • Ad Engagement & Crash Metrics: Ad impressions, ad clicks, crash logs, and frame-rate performance data.

You can inspect the privacy policies of the trusted third-party providers we may utilize:

  • Google Play Services & Google AdMob
  • Unity Ads / Unity Technologies
  • Google Firebase & Crashlytics

4. In-App Purchases & Virtual Items

Our Games may offer optional virtual goods, cosmetics, or multipliers. All financial transactions are handled exclusively by the official platform store (Google Play Billing / Apple App Store). Keneri Studios never handles, processes, or stores your credit card details or banking data. Virtual items have no real-world cash equivalence.

5. Game Saves & Local Storage

Your game progression, tycoon levels, balance, and unlocked perks are stored locally on your device or synchronized via your platform's secure cloud service (e.g., Google Play Saved Games). Keneri Studios maintains no external copy of your save data.

6. Children’s Online Privacy (COPPA & GDPR)

Our Games are built for general audiences and are not directed at children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in the European Union). We do not knowingly collect personal data from minors. If a parent or guardian believes data has been collected inadvertently, contact us immediately at keneristudios@gmail.com.

7. Managing Advertising Preferences & Opt-Outs

You have full control over personalized advertising on your mobile device:

  • Android: Go to Settings > Google > Ads > Reset or Delete advertising ID.
  • iOS: Go to Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking and toggle app permissions.
