Privacy Policy

Game: Degen Empire

Studio: Keneri Studios

Last updated: August 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Keneri Studios handles user information in the mobile game Degen Empire ("the Game").

1. Information We Collect Directly

Keneri Studios does not collect, store, or transmit your personal identity details (such as your name, personal email, phone number, address, or payment credentials). We do not host dedicated user database accounts.

2. Third-Party Services & Automated Data Collection

The Game integrates trusted third-party software development kits (SDKs) to serve advertisements and support game stability. These providers may automatically collect non-personally identifiable technical information, including:

  • Unique Advertising Identifiers (e.g., Google Advertising ID, Apple IDFA).
  • IP address and approximate geographic location (country/city level).
  • Device model, operating system version, and system language.
  • Ad performance and interaction metrics (views, impressions, clicks).

For more details on how these providers handle data, please review their privacy policies:

  • Google Play Services & Google AdMob
  • Unity Ads

3. Game Progress and Save Data

Your game progress, balances, and upgrades are stored locally on your mobile device (or synced securely through Google Play Games / Apple Game Center). Keneri Studios does not own or modify your save files on private servers.

4. Children’s Online Privacy (COPPA / GDPR)

Our game is not directed to children under 13 years of age (or under 16 in the EEA). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a minor has provided data through third-party services, please contact us so we can take appropriate measures.

5. Managing Personalized Ads

You can reset or disable ad personalization through your device settings at any time:

  • Android: Settings > Google > Ads > Delete / Reset advertising ID.
  • iOS: Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, you can contact us at:

Email: contact@keneristudios.com
