Studio Philosophy

The Keneri Standards

We reject hollow clickers and predatory game loops. Here is how we build.

Smart Gaming (Edutainment)

Games that teach compounding yield, opportunity cost, liquidity, and balance sheets while entertaining you with hyper-engaging progression.
