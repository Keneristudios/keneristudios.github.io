Cycle Tracker Privacy Policy

Absolute sovereignty: your health data never leaves your device.

Publisher / Developer: Keneri Studios

Applies to: Cycle Tracker for Android

Official Contact: keneristudios@gmail.com

Effective Date: September 2026

1. Data Stays on Your Device

Cycle Tracker stores your cycle logs, symptoms, moods, vitals and settings only in a local database on your phone. We operate no servers for health data and run no analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs. Your health data is never transmitted anywhere by this app.

2. Network Use (Two Cases Only)

  • Community (opt-in): if you join the optional community, only the content you explicitly post is sent, under an anonymous handle you choose. Health logs are never attached to community activity.
  • Premium billing: purchases are processed by Google Play. We receive only the purchase confirmation needed to unlock Pro features — never payment details.

3. On-Device Integrations

  • Health Connect (optional): sleep, exercise and weight are read only to pre-fill your daily log, stored locally, never uploaded. Revoke anytime in Health Connect settings.
  • Bluetooth smartwatch (optional): vitals are streamed to the app locally and merged into your log only when you tap Sync.
  • Notifications: period, ovulation and log reminders are scheduled and delivered entirely on-device by the system scheduler.

4. No Accounts, No Advertising

No sign-in, no profiling, no ads, no sale or sharing of personal or health data with any third party, including advertisers and data brokers.

5. Your Control / Deletion

Erase All Data in the app (Vault → Erase All Data) permanently deletes every log and resets settings on the device. Uninstalling also removes all local data. Because we hold no cloud copies, there is nothing to request from us — deletion is instant and complete.

6. Children

The app contains no social features contacting strangers by default and collects no data. Community, if enabled, is subject to Play age policies.

7. Changes

Material changes will be disclosed in-app before taking effect.
